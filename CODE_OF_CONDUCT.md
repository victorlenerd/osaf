# Code of Conduct

Afefehub is built by and for people across many countries, languages and backgrounds. We want
every contributor, reviewer and user of this project to feel welcome and safe.

## Our standards

Do:

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and across language barriers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code, not about people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.

Do not:

- Harass, insult, demean or discriminate against anyone, in any channel tied to this project.
- Publish others' private information, including anything about reviewers or businesses that
  appears in Afefehub data, without their permission.
- Post sexualised, violent or otherwise inappropriate content.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and to anyone
representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **legal@afefehub.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code,
and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone who does.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
